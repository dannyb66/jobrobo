## 3. Remove or avoid (Oct 2026)

- **Low-signal terms in your current CV:**
  - Flutter, React Native, Unity: off-target for AI, platform or backend roles. Keep them only for mobile or game roles.
  - TensorFlow: reads as dated for LLM roles.
  - "Prompt Engineering" as a lead skill: list it last.
- **Dated:**
  - Hadoop / MapReduce / Hive, jQuery, AngularJS, Chef / Puppet, Jenkins as your only CI, "SOA".
  - LangChain legacy APIs (LLMChain, AgentExecutor).
  - AutoGPT / BabyAGI.
  - OpenAI Assistants API, ChatGPT Plugins, GPT-3/3.5.
  - Rules-based chatbots (Dialogflow, Rasa).
  - BLEU / ROUGE as your main LLM metric.
- **Fluff:** "AI-first mindset", "vibe coding", "10x with AI", "autonomous AGI agents", "responsible AI champion" with no method named, "team player", "visionary leader", "familiar with", "Big Data", "cutting-edge".
- **Soft skills and PM/analytics tools:** left out of the AI-focused list (communication skills, Tableau, Amplitude, etc.).

## 5. Format rules

1. Use a single column, a plain "Skills" heading, and 5–7 labeled rows separated by commas. No tables, icons or rating bars in the resume itself.
2. Write each acronym with its full form once, e.g. "Model Context Protocol (MCP)" or "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)".
3. Copy each job posting's exact wording. For example, if it says "LLM-as-a-Judge", don't write "LLM Judging".
4. Every listed tool should show up in an experience bullet with a production result. At Staff level and above, screeners check for evals, monitoring and rollback ownership.
5. Top AI companies (Anthropic, OpenAI, Harvey, Decagon, Glean) write concepts rather than tool names. For Staff+ roles, put concept terms first and frameworks after them.
6. Put the long tail on LinkedIn Skills, and keep the resume curated per application.
